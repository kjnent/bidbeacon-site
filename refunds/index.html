Refund & Cancellation Policy

How to cancel BidBeacon Alerts, how the 14-day free trial works, and how to request a refund.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

BidBeacon Alerts subscription

  • BidBeacon Alerts is $39 per month after a 14-day free trial. If you cancel before the trial ends, you will not be charged.
  • Cancel anytime from the customer portal or by emailing us. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle, and you will not be charged again.
  • We do not provide prorated refunds for partial billing periods, except where required by law.
  • If you were charged in error, contact us within 30 days and we will refund the charge.

How to request a refund

Email hello@bidbeaconalerts.com with your name and payment details. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days.
