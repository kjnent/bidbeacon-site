How it works

1. Tell us what you bid on

Pick your states, set-aside status (small business, 8(a), HUBZone, WOSB, SDVOSB) and categories.

2. We watch SAM.gov daily

New solicitations, presolicitations and sources-sought notices are pulled from SAM.gov's official public data and filtered to your preferences.

3. You get one clear email

Each notice comes with a plain-English summary, response deadline, place of performance, a simple fit score and the link to the official notice.

Coming next: pre-filled opportunity briefs that pull the key details from a notice into a ready-to-review worksheet, so you can decide faster whether to bid.
