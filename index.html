How it works

From SAM.gov to your inbox in four steps

No more scrolling search results every morning. We do the watching; you decide what to bid.

Tell us what you bid on

Pick your states, set-aside status (small business, 8(a), HUBZone, WOSB, SDVOSB) and categories.

We watch SAM.gov daily

New solicitations, presolicitations and sources-sought notices are pulled from SAM.gov's official public data.

We filter and sort

Notices are matched to your preferences and sorted by deadline, with color-coded badges so urgent ones stand out.

You get one clear email

Each notice with a plain-English summary, response deadline, place of performance and the link to the official notice.
