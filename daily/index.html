How the daily email works

From SAM.gov to your inbox in four steps

No more scrolling search results every morning. We do the watching; you decide what to bid.

Tell us what you bid on

Pick your states, trades and set-aside status (small business, 8(a), HUBZone, WOSB, SDVOSB). New signups start with janitorial and facilities support until you choose otherwise.

We check SAM.gov every morning

New solicitations, presolicitations and sources-sought notices are pulled from SAM.gov’s official public data.

We filter and sort

Notices are matched to your preferences and sorted by deadline, with color-coded badges so urgent ones stand out.

You get one clear email

Each notice with a one-line plain-English summary, deadline, place of performance and official link, plus a heads-up when a deadline changes or an amendment posts.
