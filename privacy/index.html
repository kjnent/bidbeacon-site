Privacy Policy

How BidBeacon Alerts, a service of KJN Enterprises LLC, collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

KJN Enterprises LLC, which operates BidBeacon Alerts, respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact details you send us, such as your name, email, and business information.
  • Information needed to deliver services, such as subscription or account details you choose to share, including the states you bid in.
  • Payment information, which is handled directly by our payment processor (e.g., Stripe); we do not store full card numbers.

This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

How we use it

To respond to inquiries, provide and bill for services, send service-related alerts and notices, and meet legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate (such as payment processors and email providers), when you direct us to, or when required by law.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and use reasonable safeguards to protect it.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing us.
